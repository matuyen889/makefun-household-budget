# FinTrack Home: Intelligent Family & Household Budgeting System

An offline-first, client-side household budget management platform engineered for family cashflow clarity, 50/30/20 budget allocations, interactive expense logging, savings goals tracking, and what-if financial scenario simulations.

## Core Capabilities
- **Executive Family Overview**: Real-time headline KPIs including net monthly income, total expenses, net savings rate, and emergency runway in months.
- **50/30/20 Rule Allocator**: Automated breakdown tracking of Needs (50%), Wants (30%), and Savings/Debt (20%) with live visual gauges.
- **Dynamic Category Allocation**: SVG Donut Chart visualization across Housing, Food & Groceries, Utilities, Healthcare, Transportation, Kids & Education, Entertainment, and Personal spending.
- **Interactive Expense & Cashflow Ledger**: Multi-filter transaction manager with search, category filtering, member attribution, and local storage state persistence.
- **Family Savings Goals**: Progress meters for 6-Month Emergency Fund, College 529 Plan, Family Vacation, and Home Improvement.
- **What-If Scenario Simulator**: Dynamic sliders testing income shocks, inflation adjustments, and discretionary cuts with instant recalculation.
- **Zero Network Dependencies**: 100% offline static HTML5, CSS3, and ES Modules.
